# Accessibility

**Last reviewed:** October 2026

Waymark Gear should work for every hiker, whatever device, browser or
assistive technology they use. Our target is the
[Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/),
the standard US accessibility rules for websites point to.

## What we do

- **Keyboard.** Everything you can click can be reached and used from the
  keyboard: menus, dialogs, the list editor's buttons, filters and sliders. A
  "Skip to content" link is the first thing the Tab key reaches on every page,
  and focus is always visible.
- **Screen readers.** Pages have one main area and a heading structure; form
  fields, icon buttons and photos have names; dialogs say they are dialogs and
  keep focus inside until they close.
- **Colour and contrast.** Text meets AA contrast in both the light and dark
  themes, and links in running text are underlined rather than shown by
  colour alone.
- **Zoom and phones.** Pages fit narrow screens without sideways scrolling,
  and nothing blocks pinch-zoom.
- **Motion.** If your system asks for reduced motion, animations and
  transitions are switched off.
- **Testing.** Every change is checked by automated accessibility tests
  (axe-core) on the main pages in both themes, and the whole site has had a
  keyboard review.

## Known limitations

- **Reordering by dragging.** Items, categories and lists in the editor are
  reordered by dragging. You can still build and edit a list fully without
  dragging, but changing the order is mouse- or touch-only for now. We are
  working on a keyboard way to move them.
- **Charts.** The weight breakdown chart is a picture; the same numbers are in
  the list totals beside it.
- **Things people post.** Gear lists, photos, reviews and comments are written
  by members. We ask for descriptions but can't guarantee every photo has one.
- **Other sites.** Links to shops and brand sites take you to pages we don't
  control.

## Tell us about a problem

If something on Waymark Gear is hard or impossible for you to use, please tell
us on the [Report a problem](/report) page. Say which page it was, what you
were trying to do, and the browser or assistive technology you use if you
can. We'll reply as soon as we can and help you get what you need in the
meantime.
